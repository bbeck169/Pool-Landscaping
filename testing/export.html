Phoenix Pool Cleaning — Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my pool cleaned in Phoenix?

In Phoenix's extreme heat and high UV environment, weekly pool cleaning is strongly recommended. High temperatures accelerate algae growth and chemical degradation, meaning pools can turn green in just a few days without regular maintenance.

What does a standard weekly pool cleaning include?

Each weekly visit includes skimming the surface, brushing walls and steps, vacuuming the floor, emptying baskets, testing and balancing all chemical levels, and a brief equipment check to ensure everything is functioning properly.
