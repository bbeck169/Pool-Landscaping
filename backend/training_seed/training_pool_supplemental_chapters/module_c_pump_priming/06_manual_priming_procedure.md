# Module C — Pump Priming Diagnosis · Chapter 6: The manual priming procedure

*Original in-house authorship.*

When you need to restore prime in the field:

1. Power OFF at the breaker.
2. Close the return-side valve to prevent backflow.
3. Unscrew the pump basket lid.
4. Empty the basket of debris. Rinse.
5. Fill the pump volute + basket with clean water from a garden hose. Fill until water is at the level of the pump inlet — that's a fully flooded volute.
6. Lubricate the lid O-ring with silicone (Teflon-safe). Reseat the lid hand-tight; then snug a quarter-turn with lid wrench if provided. Do not overtighten.
7. Reopen the return-side valve.
8. Power ON at the breaker.
9. Watch through the clear lid. Water should replace air in the basket within 30 seconds. Bubbles should stop within 60 seconds.
10. If the pump primes and holds, log the visit. If not, escalate to Chapter 4 diagnostics.

**Do not run a dry pump.** A pump running without water in the volute overheats the shaft seal in about 30 seconds, destroying it. If the pump does not prime in 60 seconds, shut down and start diagnostics.
