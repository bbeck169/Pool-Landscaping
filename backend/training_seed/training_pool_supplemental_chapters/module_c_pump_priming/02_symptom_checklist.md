# Module C — Pump Priming Diagnosis · Chapter 2: Symptom checklist

*Original in-house authorship.*

Sort the symptom before you touch tools.

**Pump runs but no water flow.** Volute is not flooded. Priming issue.

**Pump runs, water flows, but bubbles in the pump basket lid.** Prime is holding at start but a small air leak upstream is bleeding air continuously. Ignore and the pump will fail to prime after the next off-cycle.

**Pump primes when cold, loses prime after running an hour.** Thermal expansion at a joint or a heat-related lid gasket problem. Progressive fail.

**Pump loses prime overnight.** Check valve failure, suction leak on the underground plumbing, or the water level dropped below the skimmer weir overnight.

**Pump never held prime since last service.** Pump basket lid gasket, lid not fully threaded, or basket-side valve left closed.

Match the symptom to a category before opening the volute — it prevents unnecessary teardowns.
