# Module C — Pump Priming Diagnosis · Chapter 1: What "prime" means

*Original in-house authorship.*

A centrifugal pool pump is **not** self-priming in the general sense — it cannot lift water from a lower elevation into an empty volute. It relies on the volute being **already flooded** with water at start-up so the impeller can throw that water outward, creating suction on the pipe. When the pump loses that flooded volute, it "loses prime" — the impeller spins in air, cannot generate suction, and the pool water never reaches the equipment pad. Common causes: suction-side air leak, water level below the skimmer weir, closed suction valve, or an empty pump basket after a service.

Understanding prime as *a flooded volute* — not *a spinning impeller* — is the mental model that unlocks every priming diagnosis in this module.
