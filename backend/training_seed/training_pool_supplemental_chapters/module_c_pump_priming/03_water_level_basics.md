# Module C — Pump Priming Diagnosis · Chapter 3: Water-level and skimmer basics

*Original in-house authorship.*

Before any mechanical diagnosis, verify the boring stuff.

**Water level.** The pool water must be at least halfway up the skimmer opening. Below that and the skimmer weir hangs above the water on down-cycles, sucking air. Homeowner leaves the auto-fill off for a week and calls "pump not working" — half the time this is the cause.

**Skimmer basket.** Full or clogged basket cuts flow and can trap air pockets. Empty and rinse.

**Skimmer valve.** Some pools have a diverter between skimmer and main drain. Verify the valve is open to the skimmer.

**Skimmer weir door.** Stuck-open weir doors slam back-and-forth on start-up and can crack. Stuck-closed weirs starve the skimmer.

**Autofill or float valve.** If installed, verify it's working. Some tenants have found a stuck-shut autofill causing repeated low-water calls.

Do these four checks before opening the pump. On my crew's data, ~40% of "pump won't prime" calls are actually one of the above.
