# Module B — Shaft Seal · Chapter 1: What a mechanical shaft seal is

*Original in-house authorship. No verbatim OEM content.*

A mechanical shaft seal keeps pool water inside the wet end of a centrifugal pump while the motor shaft spins through the volute. It has two mating faces: a **rotating face** on the shaft, and a **stationary face** pressed into the pump body. A tiny film of water separates the two faces — that film is the "seal." Springs behind the rotating half maintain constant face pressure. When the seal fails, water leaks out along the shaft between the pump and the motor. Left running, that leak floods the motor bearings and destroys the winding.
