# Module B — Shaft Seal · Chapter 4: Motor separation and impeller removal

*Original in-house authorship. Pump OEMs referenced by name + link only.*

1. Confirm power OFF and lockout in place.
2. Loosen the volute-to-seal-plate clamp band or bolts. Depending on the pump family, the motor + seal plate + impeller lifts away as one assembly, leaving the volute on the plumbing.
3. Support the motor as you separate — don't let it drop onto the shaft. A dropped shaft bends and destroys the seal.
4. Set the motor assembly on a bench with the shaft pointing up.
5. Remove the diffuser (if present) — usually two Phillips screws or a bayonet twist.
6. Hold the shaft with a wrench flat (visible from the back of the motor) or a shaft-holding tool. **Never** grip the impeller vanes with pliers — you'll deform them and unbalance the pump.
7. Unscrew the impeller counter-clockwise (LH thread on most PoolPro / Century motors — verify before applying force). Set aside on a clean rag.
8. Remove the seal plate from the motor face. Two or four bolts, brand-dependent.
9. Inspect the shaft: it should be straight, unpitted, and clean. If the shaft is grooved where the seal ran, replace the motor — the new seal will not seat properly on a grooved shaft.
