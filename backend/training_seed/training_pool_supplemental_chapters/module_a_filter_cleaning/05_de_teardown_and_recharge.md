# Module A — Filter Cleaning · Chapter 5: DE filter teardown and recharge

*Original in-house authorship. DE OEMs cited by name + link only.*

## When teardown is required

Beyond routine backwash + recharge, DE filters need a full **teardown clean** once or twice a year. Signs it's time:

- Baseline pressure creeping up despite recent backwash + recharge
- Visible DE dust returning to the pool through the returns (torn grid)
- Grinding noise from the pump (DE getting past the grids into the impeller)

## Safety first

DE dust is a respiratory hazard. Full teardown means opening a wet housing full of loose DE. Wear an N95 or half-face respirator + safety glasses for every teardown, no exceptions. OSHA classifies crystalline silica in some DE grades as a carcinogen.

## Step-by-step teardown

1. Pump OFF. Relieve pressure. Remove drain plug and let the tank empty.
2. Backwash first if possible — reduces DE mass in the tank.
3. Remove clamp band. Lift housing top clear of the grid assembly.
4. Lift the entire grid manifold as a unit. If corroded, brace with a pry bar; don't lift by a single grid.
5. Rinse each grid individually — inside and out — with a fan-spray hose. Rotate to expose all fabric surfaces.
6. Inspect each grid: tears, holes, folds, missing bumpers, cracked manifold ports. Replace any failed grid.
7. Inspect the standpipe air-bleed tube and the internal O-ring. Replace at 3-year intervals or on any sign of hardening.
8. Reassemble the manifold. Lower carefully into the tank so grids seat vertically without pinching.
9. Reseat housing top. Verify O-ring is clean and lubed with Teflon-safe silicone.
10. Tighten clamp per manufacturer torque spec.

## DE recharge quantity

Standard rate is 1 lb of DE per 5 sq ft of filter area. Read the filter label for exact filter-area rating. Under-charging leaves grids partially bare (short filter cycle). Over-charging bridges pleats and drops flow.

## Recharge method

Slurry the DE in a bucket of pool water before adding through the skimmer. Never dump dry DE directly into the skimmer — it clumps and lodges in the impeller.

## After recharge

Run pump 5 minutes to coat the grids evenly. Log the new clean-baseline pressure.
