# Module D — Jandy O-rings · Chapter 3: Tools + parts checklist

*Original in-house authorship.*

**Tools.**
- Phillips #2 screwdriver (bonnet screws)
- 5/16" nut driver (backup for some model years)
- Needle-nose pliers (O-ring lift)
- Plastic pick or wooden dowel (**never a metal pick** — it scores the O-ring groove)
- Rubber mallet (to seat the reassembled diverter without cracking the plastic)
- Silicone lube — Teflon-safe, **never petroleum-based**
- Clean rags + a shallow drip pan

**Parts.**
- The correct rebuild kit for the valve family (order by molded model number on the valve body). Kits include stem O-rings, bonnet O-ring, and the diverter seal.
- If the stem is grooved, order a replacement stem in addition to the kit.
- Optional: a spare bonnet cap. If the plastic is sun-brittle, replace it while you're already inside.

**Pre-work.**
1. Verify the pump is OFF and locked out.
2. Close upstream/downstream valves — many pads only have valves *inside* the housings; the closed diverter of the valve itself is often the only isolation. In that case, drop the water level so residual head doesn't spray you when you crack the bonnet.
3. Photograph the diverter position and handle orientation before disassembly. You'll need this to reinstall correctly.
