# Module D — Jandy O-rings · Chapter 1: The Jandy valve family

*Original in-house authorship. Jandy referenced by name + external link only. No verbatim OEM content.*

Jandy (a Fluidra brand) makes the multi-port and 2-way / 3-way diverter valves that dominate mid-market residential equipment pads. There are three families a tech will encounter regularly:

- **NeverLube diverter valves** — the black cylindrical 3-way / 2-way valves at the pad. Marketed as needing no lube; still contain O-rings that fail.
- **Space-saver valves** — a compact variant used in tight equipment areas.
- **Check valves** (JVA / Cartridge Check) — inline flapper-style anti-backflow devices with their own gasket set.

Each family uses the same core O-ring philosophy but different exact part numbers. When a tech goes on-site for a leak, the first job is identifying which valve family the customer has installed, because the seal kits are not interchangeable. Look for the molded model number on the valve body — it reads clockwise from the top and is the fastest way to order the correct rebuild kit.

The rest of this module covers **teardown of the NeverLube family** (most common) plus notes on the check-valve gasket set at Chapter 6.
