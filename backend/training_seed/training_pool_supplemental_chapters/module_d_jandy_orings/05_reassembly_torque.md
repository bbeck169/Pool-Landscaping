# Module D — Jandy O-rings · Chapter 5: Reassembly + torque

*Original in-house authorship.*

1. Lightly coat each new O-ring with Teflon-safe silicone lube. **A film, not a bead** — over-lubing attracts debris and causes premature failure.
2. Fit the shaft O-rings back on the stem. Confirm they're seated flat in their grooves; no twisted rings.
3. Reinstall the internal small O-ring first if the model uses one — reference the exploded-view diagram in the rebuild kit if you're unsure.
4. Fit the new diverter face seal to the disc. On models where the seal is bonded, install the entire replacement disc.
5. Lower the diverter assembly into the body in the position you photographed at teardown.
6. Fit the new bonnet O-ring into its groove on the bonnet. Verify it's not pinched at any point around the circumference.
7. Lower the bonnet straight down onto the body. Do not slide — sliding rolls the O-ring out of its groove.
8. Hand-thread each bonnet screw a few turns to align the bonnet, then torque in a **star pattern** (opposite sides in sequence, not around the circle). Snug only — a well-seated O-ring seals under light compression. **Do not over-torque.**
9. Reinstall the handle in the position that matches the diverter's underlying port alignment.
10. Restore water level (if lowered) and unlock the pump. Start it and watch the freshly-rebuilt joints for 60 seconds. Any weeping = disassemble and re-inspect O-ring seating.
