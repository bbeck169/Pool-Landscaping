# Module 1 — Irrigation Repairs · Chapter 2: Diagnostic sequence for a failing zone

*Original in-house authorship. Cite OEM manuals for exact valve/head specs.*

Customer says "zone 4 isn't working." Sequence:

1. **Manually activate the zone at the controller.** If it doesn't start, controller or wire fault (Chapter 6 and Module 2).
2. **Walk the zone.** If you hear water flowing but no heads pop, it's a broken lateral. If heads pop but coverage is weak, it's head/emitter issue. If nothing at all, valve failure or upstream isolation valve closed.
3. **Check the mainline pressure.** A pressure drop across the whole system points at POC or backflow.
4. **Confirm the valve is opening.** With power at the valve solenoid, either you hear the click and the diaphragm lift, or you don't. No click = solenoid dead. Click but no water = diaphragm stuck.
5. **Inspect the heads.** Broken risers, sunk pop-ups, or the classic mower-decapitated head.
6. **Check for oversprayed / underprayed zones.** A wet spot where there shouldn't be one usually points at a broken lateral just below the surface.

**Pattern to remember.** When multiple zones fail simultaneously, suspect the controller, mainline, or POC. When one zone fails, suspect that zone's valve, lateral, or heads. When *no* zones work, POC or master valve.

Two-zone-at-once failure is often the master valve or a shared solenoid common wire — check the controller wiring diagram before opening up any manifolds.
